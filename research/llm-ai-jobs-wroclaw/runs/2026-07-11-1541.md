- Several of the new cards still lack a clean canonical employer detail page, so deduplication needs another pass next run.
- EPAM's official career pages were blocked from direct fetch in this run, so the board mirror is the current public source of record for those new signals.

## Recommended Actions

- Recheck Capgemini, Link Group, Tenarai, Upvanta, Vazco, Grid Dynamics, Sii and EPAM next run for expiry or canonical-page appearance.
- Keep the expired Holisticon science lead out of the active shortlist.
- Watch whether the new board-first roles consolidate into employer pages or keep drifting across mirrors.
